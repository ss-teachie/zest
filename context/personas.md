# Teachable ICP & Persona Reference

> **Source:** Internal ICP & Persona Taxonomy (v1.0, Feb 2026). Confidential — internal use only.

## Primary Personas

Teachable operates with two parallel frameworks: **who sellers are** (persona/business model) and **what they teach** (niche/subject matter). Good campaigns combine both.

---

### Knowledge Business (KB) ★★★ Primary

**Who they are:** Experts or small teams who have built a standalone education business around their intellectual property. Courses, programs, and learning paths are the **core revenue stream** — not a side offering.

**Goals:** Grow education revenue at scale; build a product suite; establish a brand; guide students through structured learning journeys.

**Key needs:** Learning paths / curriculum sequencing, certificates, course bundles, robust analytics, professional branding, coaching integrations, upsells & order bumps.

**Typical plan:** Pro or Business. Multiple published products. Meaningful student volume (100–10,000+).

**Sub-personas:**
- **Creator-first KB** — audience-led, content-native (language teachers, YouTubers who teach)
- **Practitioner-first KB** — professional credential, compliance/cert-driven (dental CE, yoga certification, safety training)

**Copy emphasis:** Outcomes, scale, student transformation, professional credibility. Talk about building a real business — not a side hustle.

**Real examples:** Code With Mosh (tech), Elisa Azoum (French), Rahul Hyguru (health), Brent Ozar (SQL/data), Anna Geiger (childhood education).

---

### Program Distributor (PD) ★★★ Growth Bet

**Who they are:** Organizations that use Teachable to deliver training through a network of partners, franchisees, distributors, or corporate clients — not directly to individual end-learners as the primary motion.

**Goals:** Distribute training at scale (1:many); manage compliance; track completions across organizations; sell training as a B2B product or service.

**Key needs:** B2B Bulk Distribution / Organizations feature, multi-admin access, attendance tracking, compliance certificates, branded school per client, custom roles.

**Typical plan:** Business or Enterprise. High student counts in org-managed enrollments. Multi-school or partner-linked structure.

**Copy emphasis:** Scale, compliance, control, professional training infrastructure. B2B framing. Not "build your course" — "deploy training across your entire organization."

**Real examples:** Curious Refuge (AI filmmaking, B2B orgs), Tom Robins (safety training, governments).

---

### Service Amplifier ★★ Secondary

**Who they are:** Service providers — coaches, consultants, freelancers, practitioners — who use courses to scale beyond 1:1 delivery. Teachable is a tool extending their service model, not replacing it.

**Goals:** Reduce time-per-dollar; create passive income alongside active service work; package expertise into scalable products.

**Key needs:** Coaching integrations, upsells, order bumps, tiered pricing, live-to-async transition tools (drip content, time-based access).

**Typical plan:** Basic or Pro. Smaller but high-value student cohorts.

**Copy emphasis:** Moving from hours-for-dollars to scalable income. "Package your expertise." Bridge from where they are (1:1 work) to where Teachable takes them (scalable programs).

---

### Audience Builder ★ Emerging

**Who they are:** Creators and influencers whose primary identity is audience growth. Teachable is a monetization layer on top of their social presence — not necessarily the hub of their business.

**Goals:** Add a new income stream; productize knowledge without abandoning content creation; grow community.

**Key needs:** Community links, social integrations, simple product setup, bundles.

**Typical plan:** Basic or Pro. Heavy social linking (YouTube, Instagram, Facebook). Community-first school design.

**Copy emphasis:** Easy entry, monetize what you already create, grow community.

## Niche ICP Reference

Niche ICPs define **what creators teach**. Combine with persona for targeted campaigns. Use the Persona × Niche table to know which features and angles to emphasize.

| Niche | Persona Fit | Emphasize | Avoid |
|-------|------------|-----------|-------|
| Language teaching | KB creator-first | AI-assisted lesson creation, global reach, quiz tools, student engagement | Heavy compliance language |
| Yoga / fitness / wellness | KB practitioner-first | Certifications, CE credits, recurring cohorts, structured programs | Generic "online courses" language |
| Health practitioners / dental / compliance | KB practitioner-first | CE/compliance certificates, attendance tracking, professional credibility | Creator-influencer framing |
| Coding / technical skills | KB creator-first | AI course creation, technical depth, student project outcomes | Beginner-hobbyist framing |
| Business / entrepreneurship | KB + Service Amplifier | Coaching integrations, upsells, high-ticket packaging | Passive-income-only framing |
| Personal finance / investing | KB | Trust, authority, regulatory sensitivity, transformation outcomes | Hype or get-rich language |
| Personal development / coaching | Service Amplifier | 1:1-to-async scale, coaching + course bundles, student transformation | Hard product metrics |
| Safety / compliance training | Program Distributor | Bulk distribution, org-level tracking, compliance certificates | Individual creator framing |
| Kids / childhood education | KB practitioner-first | Professional branding, learning paths, parent trust | Influencer or casual creator language |

## Course Curious / Too New

Pre-qualified or early-stage users who haven't committed to a business model. Not a primary sales target. Copy should not talk down to them, but our primary pages should be optimized for KB and PD personas.

---

## Quick Copy Guide by Persona

**Writing for KB?** Lead with growing a real education business. Respect their expertise. Talk about students, not just revenue. Certificates, curriculum sequencing, and analytics matter to them.

**Writing for PD?** B2B frame. Think compliance, distribution, org-level control. They're not building a personal brand — they're deploying training at scale.

**Writing for Service Amplifier?** Acknowledge the 1:1 work they currently do. Help them see the path to passive income without abandoning what they love. Coaching + courses is the combo.

**Writing for Audience Builder?** Keep it simple and fast. They want to monetize without rebuilding their whole workflow. Community and social links matter.
